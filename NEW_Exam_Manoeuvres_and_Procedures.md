> [!danger] **Sourcing limitation applying to this whole file.** Australian primary guideline domains are **egress-blocked** (verified 2026-08-30); AMH and Therapeutic Guidelines are subscription-gated. Entries are **snippet-sourced**. Numerics appear only on three-source agreement; where diagnostic accuracy figures conflict between studies, the conflict is stated rather than resolved.

> [!note] **Three manoeuvres are deferred in from other Part A files.** `Compression Test` and `Distraction Test` were miscategorised under Gynaecology in the build list; `Femoral Stretch Test` was listed as an Orthopaedics investigation. All three are exam manoeuvres and are built here (0.12, 0.14, 0.15).

> [!warning] **A note on diagnostic accuracy figures throughout this file.** The sensitivity and specificity of physical examination tests are **highly examiner-dependent** and vary enormously between published studies — depending on whether the injury is acute or chronic, whether the patient can relax, the reference standard used, and the skill of the examiner. Figures are given where sources agree on the broad pattern; **treat them as a guide to how much weight a test deserves, not as fixed properties of the test.**
