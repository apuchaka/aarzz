> [!danger] **The legal framework in this file is jurisdiction-dependent, and that is itself the most important teaching point.** Mandatory reporting of child abuse and neglect is governed by **each Australian state and territory's own child protection legislation, and those laws are not the same**. They differ in **who must report, which types of abuse and neglect must be reported, the threshold for reporting, and to whom the report is made** — for example, in the **Northern Territory every person** must report suspected abuse or neglect, whereas other jurisdictions mandate specified professional groups including doctors; **NSW, the NT and Tasmania** mandate reporting of all five recognised types of abuse and neglect, while the **ACT** mandates a narrower set. **Doctors are mandated reporters in every Australian jurisdiction.**
> **This file therefore does not state a single reporting rule, threshold or age cut-off**, because there is no national one and stating one would be wrong somewhere. **Find your state or territory's requirements and your hospital's child protection pathway now, and know who your local child protection team is before you need them.**
